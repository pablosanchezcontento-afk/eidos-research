# Project positioning for companies and research teams

EIDOS should be evaluated as an **independent architecture and GPU-systems research program**, not as a finished foundation-model product.

## Assets demonstrated by the project

- hypothesis-driven architecture design;
- willingness to invalidate attractive results;
- GPU profiling across forward, backward and optimizer paths;
- parameter-matched baseline construction;
- causal, cache and gradient testing;
- experiment automation and sealed-evaluation design;
- large-model memory planning on consumer hardware;
- AI-agent-directed software engineering;
- technical communication for both executive and research audiences.

## Potential collaboration areas

- external reproduction on Linux or data-center GPUs;
- custom Triton/CUDA kernels for memory updates and local mixing;
- long-context benchmark design;
- scaling-law experiments;
- quality and ablation funding;
- model-training infrastructure;
- research engineering internships or mentorship.

## What a collaborator receives

A transparent research record with preserved failures, explicit claim boundaries and a concrete next-experiment queue. The value is not only a proposed architecture; it is the experimental system used to decide whether that architecture deserves further investment.
